# Terms of use

Quiet Coaching and its optional Cognitive Insights service are provided free of charge. The skill guides the communication of the assistant you use; it does not replace the terms of that service. Cognitive Insights is available by invitation and may be unavailable when its private analysis runner is offline.

Only share an excerpt when you have chosen to do so. Do not submit a full conversation or another person's private information. AI observations can be mistaken or incomplete, are not diagnoses, and should not replace your own judgment or appropriate professional advice.

See [Privacy](PRIVACY.md) for how excerpts and results are handled and [Support](SUPPORT.md) for help.
