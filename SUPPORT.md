# Support

Email [domnovoz@gmail.com](mailto:domnovoz@gmail.com) or open an issue at [github.com/domnovoz/quiet-coaching/issues](https://github.com/domnovoz/quiet-coaching/issues). We usually reply within a few working days.

**Common questions**

- **I forgot my Insights password.** There is no password recovery, because we do not collect your email. Create a new account; your old account's insights expire automatically within 90 days, or email us your username to have it removed.
- **Delete my data.** Ask your assistant to delete all insights, or delete your account at [mcp.dominiknovozamsky.com/account](https://mcp.dominiknovozamsky.com/account).
- **"The free analysis allowance for today has been reached."** The free service has a daily limit that resets at 00:00 UTC.
- **Service status.** [mcp.dominiknovozamsky.com/health](https://mcp.dominiknovozamsky.com/health)
